Why it exists

Most cash forecasts are a spreadsheet nobody checks.

Built by hand, from last year's figures and memory, rolled forward a month at a time, and never compared with what actually happened. The bank statement already knows what the council pays, to whom, and when.

How it works

Four jobs, each building on the last.

Every number comes from your own statements or from something you entered. The rules that sort them are plain and visible: no AI, nothing a treasurer cannot follow.

01

HistoryOnce, then topped up

Load three to five years of bank statements, as your bank exports them. Every line is sorted by clear rules; the few it is unsure of, you confirm once and it remembers.

02

ForecastBuild it, check it, sign it off

Each line is proposed from your history. Check it, add what history cannot know (a land sale, a pay award, your deal register) and sign it off.

03

Today's statementEach morning

Drop in the day's statement. It is sorted in seconds, joins your history, and is checked against the forecast you signed off.

04

MonitorWhenever you like

Every signed-off forecast against what actually happened, month by month, with what it got wrong and why.

What it is worth to an authority

The forecast you would build, if you had the week.

Almost none of the work is the forecast itself. It is sorting the bank lines, finding the pattern in each, and keeping it current. That part is done for you.

01

Every number explained

Each line shows the history it came from and how it is projected. Nothing is a black box.

02

The need, and when

How much you may need to borrow, by when, and whether it lasts beyond a year or recovers within it.

03

Options to discuss

Borrowing, loans falling due, LOBO call dates and cash you could lock away, worked from your forecast and your deal register.

04

A board report, ready

Seven pages for your section 151 officer, and Excel with every number behind it.

05

A few minutes a day

The morning's statement sorted in seconds, added to your history, and checked against what you signed off.

06

Made for councils

It knows the shape of a council's year: council tax in ten instalments, precepts, grants, and payroll on the last working day.
